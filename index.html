Welcome to Wolaita Zone

Located in the heart of Southern Ethiopia, Wolaita is celebrated for its fertile green valleys, dramatic mountain ranges, and rich cultural heritage.

Whether you're seeking high-altitude hiking, tranquil waterfalls, or authentic cultural festivities like Gifata, Wolaita promises an incredible travel adventure.

  • Breath-taking mountain trekking routes
  • Traditional culinary & coffee ceremonies
  • Vibrant ancient cultural heritage
