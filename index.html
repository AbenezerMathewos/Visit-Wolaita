Highlands & Peaks

Mount Damota

The Sacred Roof of Wolaita

A towering volcanic massif offering 360-degree vistas over the Great Rift Valley, Lake Abaya, and lush terraced hillsides. Revered for centuries as a sacred site of ancient kings.

⛰ Elevation 2,900 m
📍 Distance 12 km (25 min)
🌤 Best Time Oct – Feb (Dawn)
🥾 Difficulty Moderate
